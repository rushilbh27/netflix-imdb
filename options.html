Set up in three steps
  1. 1

    Get a free key from OMDb

    OMDb is the open movie database that supplies the ratings. A key is free, personal to you, and takes about a minute.

    Open the OMDb sign-up page

    Fill in their form like this:

    Account type
    FREE! (1,000 daily limit) It starts on Patreon (paid). Switch it.
    Email
    Gmail is fastest. Outlook, Hotmail and Yahoo can take up to an hour to arrive.
    First and last name
    Anything works.
    Use
    Type personal browser extension

    Then press Submit.

  2. 2

    Activate it, then copy the key

    An email from The OMDb API arrives, usually within a minute. Click the activation link first, because the key won't work until you do. Then copy the 8 letters and numbers after Here is your key:

  3. 3

    Paste it here

    Paste the key below. You can paste the whole line from the email, or the whole email, and it picks the key out.

    The key stays in this browser. It's sent to OMDb with each rating lookup and nowhere else.

Settings and troubleshooting
–lookups left today

The free key allows 1,000 lookups a day. Ratings are remembered for 30 days, so everyday browsing barely uses any.

If badges stop showing up

Netflix changes its page layout now and then, and card detection can break until the extension is updated. Turn on logging above, open the console on Netflix, and look for lines starting with [nfimdb]. Report it on GitHub and include what you see.
