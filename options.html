Netflix IMDb Ratings

Stored locally in this browser profile only. Never synced, never sent anywhere except omdbapi.com.

No key yet? Get a free one at omdbapi.com/apikey.aspx — then click the activation link OMDb emails you, or lookups will fail.

Free tier allows 1000 lookups/day. Results are cached for 30 days, so normal browsing stays well under it.

Ratings not appearing? Open the console on Netflix and run localStorage.setItem('nfimdb:debug','1'), then reload.
